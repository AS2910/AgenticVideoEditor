# Volt — the design hand-off, built (2026-10-18 → 19)

The user's words: *"Can you access `…/AgenticVideoEditor/voltage-design`? Lets build exactly like this. If needed adapt our backend systems to this … else do all of it in one shot."* The hand-off is `voltage-design/HANDOFF.md` and eleven screens (`Login, Projects, FirstRead, Main, EditorWorking, EditorDepth, Export, Voices, Usage, Settings, Billing`). This is what was built, in one branch (`volt`), and the decisions the mocks left open.

## What the hand-off is

Paper and ink with one accent: `#F3F3EF` page, `#0B0B0C` ink, **volt** `#D7FF3A` only as a fill with ink text or as text on the black pane, amber `#FFB020` for a step waiting on you. Space Grotesk for display, Geist for UI, JetBrains Mono for times and costs. 1.5 px ink borders; a hard `4px 4px 0` ink shadow on proposals, gate cards and primary buttons only; no glows, blurs or gradients. Voltage's pane is black in both themes. Light and dark are siblings; dark is the same room at night.

## What was built

**Tokens and the class layer** (`frontend/src/styles/globals.css`): the Volt tokens for `:root` and `.dark`, shadcn's roles mapped onto them, the three fonts self-hosted, and the hand-off's vocabulary as classes — `.btn(.volt .ink .ghost .sm .lg .shine)`, `.act`, `.pill`, `.sugg`, `.kbd`, `.stamp(.need .in .inkd)`, `.row`, `.prop`, `.waiting`, `.segs/.seg`, `.pane/.pm/.bubble/.trace/.gate/.wave`, `.meter`, `.lane-n`, `.blk`, the keyframes. `theme.test.ts` knows the new tokens and still forbids raw colours, gradients, blurs, `shadow-[`, text under 12 px, `vh`, radii past `xl`.

**Routing** (`src/lib/route.ts`): one hash route — `#projects`, `#pN`, `#pN/export`, `#voices`, `#usage`, `#settings`, `#billing`; `useRoute`, `hrefFor`, `go`. `App.tsx` is the shell: it opens the project a route names, lets it go on `#projects`, and renders the page.

**The editor** (`App.tsx`, `Player`, `LineDoc`, `VoltagePane`, `WhoSpeaks`, `Lanes`, `Wordmark`): the header as `Main.dc.html` draws it; the picture with mode tag, clock, caption, scan line and big play on the first read; the transport row with markers; **Tracks ▾** revealing the lanes and Precise; the transcript as `.row`s with `.who` + stamp, `.say`, `.meta` and the verbs (Hear it ␣ · Add it K · Edit it · Discard; Hear it · Take it out · Edit it on a line in the video; hover verbs on a line as shot); **Edit it opens the depth in place** (`EditorDepth`: Before/After, delivery pills, direction, facts grid, Measured tiles with meters, lip-sync not measured yet, Voice it again, Change the words, Less ↑); runs of untouched lines fold to "+ N more lines"; the pane as turns (bubble, trace with tool and cost per step, reply, gate card with THIS STEP / LEFT IN TURN, earlier-turn chips, suggestion chips, composer with the turn cap). The first read: Who's speaking (Voltage's guess per speaker, lines and seconds, Rename, ✓ Looks right) and, on a silent clip, what Voltage saw with the place to confirm or correct. While Voltage works: the scan line and its step on the picture, `Waiting on you` on the row it stopped at.

**The pages** (`src/pages/`, by a subagent): `Shell` (header, `.nav`, account circle), `ProjectsPage` (drop zone, the sample, cards with Open / Delete-with-undo), `VoicesPage` (your voices; record with MediaRecorder or drop a file; clone a speaker; design three previews; keep what Voltage made; one `<audio>` for Hear it), `UsagePage` (week / month / all), `SettingsPage` (defaults, what still asks under Just do it, appearance, sign out, delete everything), `BillingPage` (honest placeholder), `LoginPage`, `ExportPage` (renders once if needed, Download, Copy link, the receipt). 31 tests.

**The backend** (`backend/app`, by a subagent; 667 tests green): `GET/PUT /me/settings` and `ask_before` on project settings (the agent's `ship`, `clone_speaker`, `design_voice` gates honour it; `GET /projects/{id}/settings` with the read-only model); step messages carry `data={"tool","t"}`, `WaitingForUser.cost_usd` and the question's `cost_usd` / `left_usd`; `GET /usage?period=`; `GET /voices` with `preview_url`, `kind`, `sample_sha`, `used_in`, `slots`; `GET /voices/sample/{sha}`, `GET /voices/made`, `POST /voices/mine/keep`, `GET /voices/speakers`, `POST /voices/mine/from-speaker`, `POST /voices/design`, `POST /voices/mine/from-preview`; `DELETE /account`; `GET /projects/{id}/receipt` (and `_export` now records the last final render); the approved-edit summary carries `speaker` and `voice_profile_id`.

**Gone**: `ChatPanel`, `ReadingCard`, `ShipSheet`, `LoadScreen`, `ProjectList`, `SignIn`, `VoicesSheet`, `SpeakersBar`, `ExportBar`, `SpendMeter`, `PlanCard`, `ActivityLog`, `AutonomySwitch`, `ThemeToggle`, `Presence`, `Avatar`, the dock and the rail. The plan card's jobs live on the rows (Voice it again, the depth) and in the trace.

## Decisions the mocks left open

- **Billing** is a placeholder with real ceilings and no payment flow; the copy says so. No "Change plan" / "Update card".
- **Recording** uses the browser's MediaRecorder; when it is absent the button is disabled and the copy reads "Drop a file".
- **Take switcher** (the depth's "takes" segments) is omitted until takes are stored per item; "Voice it again" asks Voltage for another.
- **Suggestion chips send** (as the mock's `.sugg` implies) rather than fill the box; after the first turn they go.
- **Self-ship under Just do it** no longer opens a sheet: a note in the pane says what went in, with Export → and the download; the receipt is the export page.
- **Status words**: `Proposed`, `✓ In the video`, `Waiting on you`, `Removed`, `Voicing…`, `Planned`.
- **Edit it on a proposal** opens the depth (as `EditorDepth` shows); the words are changed from there ("Change the words").
- **Speaker voices by hand** (the old SpeakersBar select) are asked of Voltage in words; `PUT /speakers/{label}` with `voice_id` stands.
- **A variant** keeps its route but has no button in these screens.
- **Theme** is chosen on Settings (Appearance); the editor header carries no toggle, as drawn.
- **Fonts** are self-hosted (`@fontsource-variable/space-grotesk|geist|jetbrains-mono`); `npm install` is needed in the main checkout after the merge.
- **Type floor** stays 12 px; the mock's 11 px tags became 12.
