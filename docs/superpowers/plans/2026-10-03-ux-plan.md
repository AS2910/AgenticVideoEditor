# The UX/UI plan, against the user stories

**Date:** 2026-10-03 · **Status:** proposed. Built from the user stories agreed the same day (A1–I2, listed at the end), by walking every screen of the current app against them. Nothing here is built yet.

## Two findings that change the shape of the product

**1. The line is the unit, and the app does not treat it that way.** Every story in B, C, E and F is about *one line*: change it, hear it, judge it, pick a take, choose how it meets the picture, undo it. Today a line's take appears in the right-hand panel, far from the line; its approval, revert and "how it fits" live in three different places; the panel juggles three jobs in one text box. The fix is architectural, not cosmetic: **everything about a line happens at the line**, in the transcript, and the panel is for the whole video — intent, the plan, questions, the receipt.

**2. The picture must not stop.** An added line holds the frame today because the engine's "add after" is the only mode the agent uses. Story F1 is unambiguous: the picture keeps playing under an added line, and a freeze is the user's call, never the default. "Play over" already exists in the engine; it is never offered. This is the first thing to fix.

## Principles

- **At the line.** Edit, take, verdict, approve, undo, fit and voice are all shown on the line they belong to.
- **Plain words, then numbers.** Every verdict reads as a sentence first ("sounds like him, a touch fast"); the score is behind it.
- **Nothing happens to the picture without saying so.** Every change that touches the picture (play over, hold, slow) is named on the line and chosen there.
- **One action, last.** Whatever the user must do next is the final element in the panel, with history above it. (Already in place; kept as a rule.)
- **Ask once, remember.** Consent, voices, fit preferences and autonomy are asked when they first matter and then remembered per project.
- **Cost before, receipt after.** Every paid step shows its cost before it starts and its cost after it finishes.

## The design

### 1. Information architecture

Three regions, each with one job:

- **The script** (left, the whole height): the transcript as the working surface. Monitor above it, docked small; the word timeline hidden behind "Precise" (H1). Every line carries its own state and its own controls.
- **Voltage** (right): the conversation about the *whole video* — goal, findings, question, plan, progress, receipt — and nothing about an individual line except as a link to it.
- **The bar** (top): project, spend against the cap, Ship.

### 2. The line, in full (G7–G13, G17, G22)

A line is a row: time · speaker · words · state. Hovering or focusing it reveals its actions; opening it shows its editor. Everything below lives *inside the row*.

- **States**, as chips in words: *Planned*, *Voicing…*, *Ready to hear*, *Needs you*, *Shipped*, *Held*.
- **Actions** (hover / focus / keyboard): *Change the words*, *Change the delivery*, *Add a line after*, *Remove*, *Play original*.
- **The editor** (open): wording with tracked changes as you type; **Delivery** (as spoken / warmer / more excited / calmer / slower — a sentence to the model, not a slider); **Voice** (the speaker's); **Sound meets picture** for added lines: *Play over the picture* (default) / *Hold the picture* / *Replace what's there*; **If it runs long**; cost and time ("≈ 40 characters · 2¢ · about 10 s") before *Hear it*.
- **The take**, inline under the line: a play button that plays *the line in the video* (picture and all, from a beat before), the verdict as a sentence with the score behind it, and two buttons: *Keep* and *Another take*. A second take appears beside the first as a choice, not a replacement (C3).
- **Kept**: the line shows its tracked changes with *Undo* in the margin, which works at any time (C5).
- **Removed** (B5): the words struck through, the gap closed with room tone; a chip says *Removed*; Undo brings it back. Cutting the picture too is a later option.
- **Errors** appear in the row they belong to, in one sentence with one action (G16).

### 3. Adding a line (G8, G6, F1)

"Add a line after" opens an empty editor between rows with *Sound meets picture* defaulting to **play over the picture**. The engine's "layer" mode carries it; "hold the picture" is the old "add after", one click away and named for what it does. On a clip with no speech, the goal stage becomes "Where should the voice-over go?": the monitor with a draggable range and the same editor.

### 4. The goal stage shows the clip (G3–G5)

Left: the read transcript, compact, with the speakers named by role as Voltage guesses them ("the Customer", "the Shopkeeper") and a one-click confirm or rename. Right: the goal box as now. Voltage's opening line becomes specific: "Two people, twenty lines; the offer is never said; the brand name is said once, at 0:07."

### 5. The panel's three jobs become two (G18–G21)

- The box is for the **whole video**: a goal, or a change to the plan. Line edits happen at the line, so they leave the box.
- **Changing the plan in words edits the plan in place** (B8): "not the second one" unticks it; "shorter at 0:17" rewords it; the takes already voiced survive. This needs a planner call that returns a *diff of the plan*, not a new plan.
- An open question is answered by its chips, or by typing; the box says so.
- A running plan gets **Stop**; what is done stays done.
- Plan items get the same **Sound meets picture** and **Delivery** controls the line editor has.

### 6. Review becomes per-line (G23–G25)

A list of changed lines, each with its own small player (the line in the video, before / after on a toggle), the verdict sentence, and **Keep / Hold** per row. *Ship* ships what is kept and holds the rest as drafts (C6). After shipping, every line keeps *Undo*, and the receipt lists what shipped with the cost.

### 7. Finishing (G26–G28)

*Ship* opens a sheet: what is in the file (lines changed, added, removed; length before and after), a download, a link, and *Make a variant* (duplicates the project with its plan as a draft). The project list shows a frame, the state (draft / shipped), and the last change.

### 8. Trust and money (G1, G31, D2–D4)

- Consent is asked once per project, the first time a voice is about to be synthesised, in a sentence: "I'll be creating speech in this person's voice. You have their permission?" — and remembered.
- Spend is a small meter against the project's cap; every paid step shows its cost before it starts (line editor, plan foot) and after (take, receipt).
- "Invented speech" never reaches the UI (built 2026-10-03); a clip with nothing to change says so.

### 9. Voice and picture, when their phases land (G29, G30)

The line editor's *Voice* gains *their own voice* (Phase 15) and *perform it yourself* (record on the mic, Voice Changer puts it in their voice). *Sound meets picture* gains *ease the picture* (Phase 16). On-camera lines get *match the mouth* (Phase 17). The controls exist now with the options that are real; the rest appear when they are.

### 10. Keyboard (H3)

Up/down moves between lines, Enter opens the editor, Esc closes it, Space plays the line, K keeps, U undoes, / focuses the goal box.

## Backend needed

- **Mix per line in the plan**: the planner returns `mix` with `layer` as the default for added lines over moving picture; `concatenate` only when asked. The run and review respect it. (Small.)
- **Remove a line**: a new mix `remove` — the span's dialogue replaced with room tone (the stems work in Phase 15 makes this clean under music); the picture untouched. (Medium.)
- **Delivery**: a `delivery` instruction per line passed to the voice (ElevenLabs v3 audio tags / prompt; stability and style settings) and to the shorten prompt. (Medium.)
- **Plan diff**: `POST /plans/{id}/revise {instruction}` — the planner returns changes to items (untick, reword, add, mix) applied in place. (Medium.)
- **Two takes**: the adapter already voices several takes by duration; keep the runner-up as a second candidate when asked. (Small.)
- **Stop a plan**: a cancel flag the job checks between items. (Small.)
- **Consent per project at first voicing**: already enforced server-side; the UI moves. (None.)
- **Variants**: duplicate a project with its plan as a draft. (Small.)

## Open decisions

1. **Monitor placement.** Docked small above the script (this plan) or large with the script beside it (today). Small keeps the line as the unit; large suits review. Proposal: small while editing, large in Review.
2. **Remove = mute or cut?** Mute (room tone, picture untouched) first; cut (picture too) with Phase 16.
3. **Delivery vocabulary.** A short fixed set (as spoken / warmer / more excited / calmer / slower / firmer) plus "in your words". Proposal: the fixed set, with free text behind "more…".
4. **Where the receipt lives after shipping.** In the panel (today) or in the ship sheet. Proposal: both, same component.
