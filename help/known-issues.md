Voltage is in beta. These are the gaps we know about today, said plainly. If you hit something that is not here, send us feedback from Help.

## Voices

- **Cloning a voice is closed for now.** Making a voice from your own recording or from a speaker in the video will open with a read-aloud consent check, which is still being tried out. Describe a voice instead. If you need cloning for the beta, write to us and we can turn it on for your account.

## Working in the editor

- **Renders take a while.** A preview or an export of a 60 s video takes several seconds, and longer when many people are working at once. You can keep working while it renders.
- **Reviewers can watch but not comment** unless review is turned on for your account. Beta accounts have it on.

## Money

- **Prices and plans may change after the beta.**

## Everything else

- **The Terms, Privacy and Acceptable use pages are drafts.** They will be replaced before Voltage opens to everyone.
- **The sample video is a stand-in.** A real sample is on its way.
