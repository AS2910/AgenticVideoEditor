title: What Voltage counts
section: Account and privacy
order: 906
---
Voltage counts the steps you take, not what is in your videos, so we can see where people get stuck.

**What is counted.** Each key step is one event: signing in, uploading a video, asking Voltage, a take voiced, a take added, an export, and the project steps (a brief saved, a check run, a review link opened, and so on). An event names the step, your account, the video or project it touched, and small facts such as a length in seconds or a count. The words of your lines, briefs and comments are not copied into events.

**What it is used for.** A few numbers on an internal page: how many people reach each step from signing up to a first export, how many voiced takes are added, and what an exported minute costs. Voice quality scores per voice service appear there only when a take's score was measured; nothing is estimated.

**Who sees it.** Only the people who run this Voltage. It is never shown to other customers and never sold.
