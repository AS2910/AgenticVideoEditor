---
title: Signing in
section: Account and privacy
order: 901
---
Sign in with Google, or with a link sent to your email. Your videos and voices are visible only to you and the people you invite.

When sign-in is on, **Continue with Google** is the door. Below it, **Or sign in with your email**: type your address and choose **Email me a link**. The link works once, for 15 minutes. If you have signed in with Google using the same address, the link opens that same account.

Everything you upload, every voice you make and every charge is tied to your account (or to the workspace you work in, when you were invited into someone's). Sign out from Settings.
