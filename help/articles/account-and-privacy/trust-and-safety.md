---
title: Consent, public figures and marked exports
section: Account and privacy
order: 907
---
Voltage clones a voice only with the speaker's consent, never a public figure's, and marks every export.

**Read a sentence aloud.** Before a speaker's voice is cloned, they read a short sentence with a code in it, recorded in the browser or uploaded. It shows they are there and agree. The recording is kept with the consent record. In this build the check runs in test mode: the recording is kept but the words are not checked yet.

**No public figures.** Voltage will not clone a voice named after a known public figure, such as a politician or a film star. The line uses a voice from the catalogue instead and says why.

**Marked exports.** Each export carries an invisible mark and a provenance record saying the voice was made by Voltage, so a copy found elsewhere can be traced. In this build the mark is in the file's details and the record is signed with a test key.

**Report misuse.** Anyone can report a voice used without consent or anything harmful, from a shared link or from Help, without an account. We read every report.

**Your data.** Settings has Download my data (a zip of every record of yours, with a list of your media) and Delete my data, which deletes your account and everything in it. Terms, Privacy and Acceptable use are linked at the foot of Settings and Help; they are drafts until legal review.
