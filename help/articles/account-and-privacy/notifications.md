The bell at the top right says when something needs you, and you choose what is emailed.

**What the bell shows.**

- **Long work finished**: an export, a project check, a batch, an import or another job that took more than a minute is done. Short work and each voiced line are not announced; you see those where you are.
- **Something failed**: work that stopped with an error, with the reason. A line that failed inside a turn shows in that turn instead.
- **Review comments, approvals, changes asked for**: what your reviewers did through a review link.
- **Spend alerts**: a video, a project or the month reached 50%, 80% or 100% of its ceiling. Each is said once; raising the ceiling starts the count again.

**Reading them.** The number on the bell is how many are unread. Choose the bell to see the latest; choose one to open the video or project it is about, or **Mark all read**. The count updates by itself every 20 seconds or so.

**Your settings.** Choose **Notification settings** at the bottom of the list (or **Settings**, then **Notifications**). For each kind, turn **In app** and **Email** on or off. Email never comes one by one: everything waiting goes in one email, at most every 10 minutes, the same digest that carries review news. Long work finished is off by email unless you turn it on.

Settings are yours alone: people you invite choose their own.
