---
title: Deleting a video or a voice
section: Account and privacy
order: 903
---
Deleted things wait in the Bin for 30 days. Restore them from there until then; after that they go for good.

Delete a video from Home (Undo is offered for a few seconds). Delete a voice of yours from the Voices page. When the 30 days are up, the video's media, takes and renders go, and the clone made from its speaker is deleted at the voice service. The consent record is kept for the legal retention period. Charges stay in Usage under the item's name.

Delete everything, in Settings, removes every video, voice and record of your account at once, without the Bin.
