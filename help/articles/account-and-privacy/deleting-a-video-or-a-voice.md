---
title: Deleting a video or a voice
section: Account and privacy
order: 903
---
Deleting removes the media, the takes, any clone made from it and the consent record.

Delete a video from Home (Undo is offered for a few seconds). Its media, takes, renders and the clone made from its speaker go with it. Delete a voice of yours from the Voices page. Delete everything, in Settings, removes every video, voice and record of your account.
