---
title: Withdrawing consent
section: Account and privacy
order: 904
---
Delete the voice or the video to withdraw consent. It goes to the Bin, where it cannot be used, and after 30 days the clone is deleted at the voice service.

Consent to clone a speaker is recorded with the video. Deleting the video, or the kept voice, stops every use of the clone at once and deletes it at the voice service when the Bin empties. The consent record itself is kept for the legal retention period. Delete everything, in Settings, removes it all at once.
