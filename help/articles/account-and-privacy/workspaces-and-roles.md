Invite people into your workspace as Editors or Reviewers, and give each a monthly spend limit.

**Roles.**

- **Owner**: you, in your own workspace. Everything, including members and billing.
- **Editor**: everything except members and billing: uploads, turns, edits, projects, exports.
- **Reviewer**: watches every video and project and comments on videos. Changes nothing.

**Invite someone.** Open **Settings**, then **Members**. Under **Invite someone** type their email, pick **Editor** or **Reviewer**, optionally a monthly spend limit, and choose **Send invite**. They get an email with a link; you can also **Copy link** from the waiting list and send it yourself. An invite works once and for 14 days; **Turn off** stops it at once. A new invite to the same address replaces the older one.

**Accepting.** The link shows whose workspace it is and the role. They sign in (Google, or an emailed link) and choose **Accept invite**; from then on they work in your workspace. What they make is yours, in your projects and your Usage.

**Spend limits.** Whatever a member starts (turns, voices, checks) counts against their own monthly limit, inside the workspace's ceilings. When they reach it, paid steps stop with a sentence asking them to ask you to raise it. **Members** shows each person's spend this month against their limit; change the limit in their row, or clear it for none.

**Change or remove.** Change a role with the select in their row. **Remove** takes them out of the workspace; what they made stays.

**More than one workspace.** Someone in several workspaces picks the one to work in under **Working in** on the Members page.

**Sign-in off.** On a Voltage with sign-in off there is one account, and it is the Owner. Invites are kept and work once sign-in is on.
