Every video in a project has a **stage**: **Draft**, **In review**, **Changes asked**, **Approved** or **Exported**. A new video starts in Draft.

On the project page, choose **Board** beside **Grid** to see the videos as columns, one per stage. Each card shows the first frame, the length, how many review comments are still open, and **Brief changed** or **Out of sync** when they apply. The page remembers which view you picked.

**Moving a card.** Drag it to another column. Or, with the keyboard or on a phone, select **Move to…** on the card and pick the stage. Each move is written to the activity log once, with who moved it and when.

**Moves that happen for you.**

- Sending a review link for a video (or for the whole project) puts it **In review**.
- A reviewer who approves through their link makes it **Approved**, under their name. A reviewer who asks for changes makes it **Changes asked**.
- A batch export that makes every file of a video moves it to **Exported**.
- **A new edit on an Approved video takes it back to Draft**, because what was approved is no longer what is in the video. Before you add a line to an Approved video, the editor says "This video is Approved. Adding this moves it back to Draft." Choose **Add anyway** to add it, or **Cancel** to leave the video as it was approved. **Add all that passed** on a batch asks the same once for every Approved video in it. When Voltage is about to add lines to an Approved video, it asks you first, even under Just do it.

**Activity.** The Activity panel on the project page lists what happened, newest first, in plain sentences: who added or took out lines, who moved a video, who approved it, who sent a review link. It updates by itself while you work; **Show older** reads further back.
