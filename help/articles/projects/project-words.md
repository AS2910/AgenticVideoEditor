title: Project words, banned words and required lines
section: Projects
order: 1102

The Words section on a project page sets the way a brand or a name is said, the words no video says, and the lines every video carries. Voltage reads them in every turn and checks each take against them.

Words: each term has how to say it ("BAH-jee cam"), an optional respelling, and an optional short clip of it said right (up to 10 seconds). The respelling is what the voice is sent in place of the term, so it is said the project's way; the line keeps the term as written everywhere you read it.

Before a take is offered, its own words are checked against the project's words. A take where a term did not come out as written (or as its respelling) is marked "Breaks a word rule", with what the take said. Voltage does not add it or offer it as its pick; voice it again, or add it yourself if it sounds right to you.

Banned words: a take Voltage wrote with one is marked the same way. A line you type with one is only warned about ("You can still voice it"); it is never blocked, because the words are yours.

Required lines: lines like "T&C apply" that every video should carry. Voltage reads them in every turn; the project check looks for videos missing one.

Learn a word: in a video that is in a project, select a word in the transcript and choose "Add to project words". Fill in how it is said, and optionally a respelling and a short clip.
