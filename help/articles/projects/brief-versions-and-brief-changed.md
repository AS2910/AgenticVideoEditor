---
title: Brief versions and "Brief changed"
section: Projects
order: 1101
---
Every save of a project's brief is a new version. A video made with an older one shows "Brief changed", and Bring up to date fixes only what the change touches.

The brief card on the project page names the version Voltage reads now, who saved it and when, for example "v4 · edited by you, 2 days ago". History lists every version; pick any two to see the words that went (struck through) and the words that came in (highlighted). Accepting one of Voltage's brief suggestions saves a new version too.

Each video remembers the brief version its last added line was made with. When the brief changes, every video made with an older version shows a "Brief changed" chip on its card, on Home and in the editor's header. A video that has nothing added yet is never marked: nothing in it was made with any brief. A video moved in from elsewhere with lines already added counts as made before this project's brief.

The project page then says how many videos were made with an older brief, with Bring up to date. Voltage takes them one at a time. First it checks, for free, whether any word the brief change added or removed appears in the video's lines; a video the change does not touch is marked current without spending anything, and the page says why. For the others Voltage runs a normal turn with the goal "Apply brief changes v3 to v4" and the change itself, proposes only the lines the change touches, and stays inside each video's turn cap. Lines it proposes wait for your Add it as usual; a video counts as current once they are in it. Each video's progress shows in its Activity.
