A template starts a new project with everything a campaign shares already set, so the next one does not begin from nothing.

**What a template keeps.** The brief (its current version), the project words and rules (how to say a term, banned words, required lines like "T&C apply.", style rules), the export presets, the defaults for new videos (how much Voltage may do, money per turn, effort) and the project ceiling. Videos are never copied: a project made from a template starts empty.

**Save one.** On a project page, choose **•••** then **Save as template…**, name it and choose **Save template**. An archived project can be saved too.

**Voices.** A template links voices, it never makes them again. A voice that is already in your voices is linked as it is; if you later delete it, new projects from the template leave it out. A voice that belongs to the project asks the same question as moving a video out: **Copy the voice into the template**, or **Keep it in my voices, for any project**.

**Start a project from one.** On Home, choose **+ New project**, name it and pick a template under **Start from**. Blank starts with nothing set.

**Starters.** Three templates come with Voltage: **Festive ad**, **Product explainer** and **Interview cleanup**. Each has a brief, a few rules, presets and defaults. They are read-only: start a project from one, change what you like, then save that as your own.

**Rename or delete.** Choose **Templates** at the bottom of Home's sidebar. Your own templates can be renamed or deleted there; projects already made from a template keep everything they have.
