A **review link** lets a client watch a video, leave comments at the moment they are about, and approve it or ask to change it. They need no account; the link is the key.

**Send for review.** In the editor, choose **Send for review** in the header. On a project page, choose **Send for review** beside the videos and tick the ones to send (all of them to start). Add the reviewer's name and email if you like, pick how long the link works (30 days unless you change it), and leave **Allow download** off unless they need the file. Choose **Make the link**, then **Copy link** and send it however you like. Sending a link puts each video **In review**.

**What the reviewer sees.** The latest finished version of each video (Export renders one; so does a preview without proposals). A version picker lists every version kept, each with **What changed**: the lines that differ from the version before, in plain words. Nothing else of the project is shown: no voices, no costs, no brief. With download off there is no Download button and the reviewer's name sits over the picture.

**Comments.** The reviewer pauses where something should change and writes a comment; it is pinned to that time in that version. **Approve** moves the video to **Approved** under their name. **Ask for changes** moves it to **Changes asked**. Either can carry a note.

**In the editor.** Each comment is a mark on the scrubber at its time and a row in the **Comments** list under the transcript. Select its time to jump there. **Reply** (the reviewer sees it), **Resolve** when it is done, or **Do what the client asked**, which hands the comment and its time to Voltage as a normal turn. The board shows how many comments are still open on each card.

**Email.** You get one email with everything your reviewers said, at most every 10 minutes. A reviewer who gave an email hears when a new version is ready. Until an email provider is set up, these emails are kept in the app's outbox instead of being sent.

**Turning a link off.** Open **Send for review** again: every link for the video is listed with its state. **Turn off** stops it at once; whoever opens it is told the link was turned off. An expired link says so too. Comments already made stay.
