Deleting a project, a video or one of your voices moves it to the **Bin**. It waits there for **30 days**, then goes for good.

**Open the Bin** from **Bin** in the bar at the top. Each item shows what it is, who deleted it and when, and how many days it has left. An item with 3 days or fewer is marked in amber. Use the tabs to see only projects, videos or voices.

**Restore.** Choose **Restore** on the item.
- A **video** goes back into its project. If that project is in the Bin too, or is gone, the video comes back as a standalone video, and the message says so. You can move it into a project any time.
- A **project** comes back with the videos that were deleted with it. A video you deleted on its own before stays in the Bin until you restore it.
- A **voice** comes back to your voices and can be picked again.

Everything made on an item comes back with it: its turns with Voltage, its edits, its takes and its renders.

**While it is in the Bin** nothing can use it. A deleted voice cannot be picked in the editor, on the Voices page or by Voltage. A deleted video or project is hidden from Home, the project pages and search.

**After 30 days** the item, its media and its takes are removed, and a voice made for it is deleted at the voice service. What it cost stays in **Usage** under its name. The consent record of a voice, or of a speaker whose voice was cloned, is kept after that for the legal retention period.

**Deleting a project** asks what to do with its videos: delete them too (they go to the Bin with it and come back with it), or keep them as standalone videos.
