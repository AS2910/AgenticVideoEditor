title: What Voltage has learned
section: Projects
order: 1112

Voltage learns how you like your lines from what you do with the ones it proposes, one project at a time.

**What it watches.** Each time you add a proposed line, discard one, or change its words before adding it, Voltage notes it, with the words before and after. After you discard a line you can tell it why (Wrong words, Wrong voice, Too long, Not needed); that is optional.

**Every 10 decisions.** After every 10 decisions in a project, Voltage looks for one clear pattern and may suggest one line: something for the brief, or a style rule such as "Keep lines under eight words." It shows on the project page under **What Voltage has learned**, with what it saw. This look is one small Claude call, charged to the project and shown in Usage. Without a Claude key, or with the project at its ceiling, it is skipped.

**Nothing changes until you say so.** Choose **Add to brief** (the brief gets a new version with the line at the end) or **Add as a rule** (a new style rule in the project's words). **Not now** leaves it out; Voltage looks again after the next 10 decisions.

**Take it back out.** Everything you added this way is listed under **What Voltage has learned**. **Remove** takes it out again: a brief line goes in a new brief version without it, a rule is removed. From the next turn on, Voltage no longer reads it.
