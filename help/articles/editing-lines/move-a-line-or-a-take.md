title: Move a line or a take
section: Editing lines
order: 302

Drag a line by its grip or type a new start time. Moving never voices again, so it costs nothing.

Every movable row has a grip on its left. Drag it up or down the transcript, press Enter on it to type a time, or use Shift to a time… in the row's actions. A proposal moves with its take; a line in the video is re-placed in one step. Nothing is voiced again.
