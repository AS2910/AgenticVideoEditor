---
title: Change, add or remove a line
section: Editing lines
order: 301
tour: add
---
Click a line and choose Edit it, or ask Voltage in words. A removed line is filled with the room's own sound.

Hover a line as shot for Hear it, Edit it, Discard and Add a line after. Edit it opens the words to change; Enter sends them to Voltage as a sentence and the take comes back as a proposal on the row. Discard proposes removing the line: the gap is closed with the room's own sound, and the picture is untouched. Add a line after adds a new line, over the picture by default, or holding the picture while it plays.
