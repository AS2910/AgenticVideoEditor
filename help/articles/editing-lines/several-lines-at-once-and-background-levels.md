Added lines can play at the same moment. Set one to background and it sits 12 dB under the words.

Lines at the same moment are separate rows and play together; the transcript says "with N other lines at this moment". On an added line or a proposal, Make it background mixes it 12 dB under whatever plays there; Make it foreground brings it back. Changing the level never voices again.
