section: Voices

When nothing fits, Voltage designs a voice from a description and gives you three previews. It cannot hear them, so you choose.

A design costs about six cents and returns three previews in the pane. Listen and press Use this on one; Voltage saves it to the video and voices the line with it. You can also design one on the Voices page and keep it as yours.
