---
title: Recording your own voice
section: Voices
order: 405
---
Record or upload at least 10 seconds, confirm it is you, and say "in my voice" in any video.

On the Voices page, press the record button or drop a file of you speaking (ten seconds or more, in a quiet room), name it, and tick the consent line. Then say "in my voice" to Voltage and the line is voiced with it.
