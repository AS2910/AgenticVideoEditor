Press Keep in my voices on any voice made in a video. Kept voices work everywhere; ask for them by name.

Voices Voltage makes inside a video (designed, cloned, added from the library) are listed on the Voices page under What Voltage made. Keep one under a name and it becomes yours: ask for it by name in any video, or share it into a project so every video there can use it.
