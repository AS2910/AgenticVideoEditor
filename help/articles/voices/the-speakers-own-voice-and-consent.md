title: The speaker's own voice and consent
section: Voices

Voltage clones a speaker from their own speech once you confirm you have the right to. Deleting the video withdraws it.

When a changed line should sound like the person who said it, Voltage clones that speaker from their own speech in the clip (it needs about ten clean seconds). The first time, it asks you to confirm you may edit and clone this speaker; that consent is recorded with the video. Deleting the video deletes the clone at the vendor too.
