Voltage clones a speaker from their own speech once you confirm you have the right to. Deleting the video withdraws it.

When a changed line should sound like the person who said it, Voltage clones that speaker from their own speech in the clip (it needs about ten clean seconds). The first time, it asks you to confirm you may edit and clone this speaker; that consent is recorded with the video. Deleting the video moves it to the Bin; after 30 days the clone is deleted at the vendor too, and the consent record is kept.

Before the clone is made, the speaker reads a short phrase aloud, so a voice is never cloned from a clip alone. Voltage checks the words and compares the voice with the speaker in the video; a close call goes to a person at Voltage. Until the check passes, their lines use a voice from the catalogue and say why. See Consent, public figures and marked exports for what each result means.
