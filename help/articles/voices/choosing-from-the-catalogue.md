title: Choosing from the catalogue
section: Voices
order: 402
---
Ask for a kind of voice, an accent, an age or a mood, and Voltage picks the closest and says why.

Say what you want in words: "an Indian woman, warm and unhurried", "a younger man". Voltage searches the catalogue and your own voices, says which it chose and why, and voices the line. To change it, say so: "use Brian", "something calmer".
