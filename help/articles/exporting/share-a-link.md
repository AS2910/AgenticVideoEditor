title: Share a link
section: Exporting
order: 702
---
Copy a private link to the exported file from the export page.

Copy link on the export page puts the file's link on your clipboard. With sign-in on, the link works for people in your workspace.

For a whole project's exports, **Share link** under Export videos on the project page makes a link anyone can open without an account, for 30 days. See Export many videos at once.
