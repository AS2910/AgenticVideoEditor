section: Fitting a line
order: 602
---
In order: a tighter wording, another take, borrowing from pauses, then easing the picture by up to 12 percent.

The fit ladder is: trim pauses first (gaps), then let the picture flex a little (up to 12 percent slower or faster), then a tempo change on the voice. When a line cannot fit, Voltage asks you: a shorter wording, or let it run into the pause after it.
