title: What the verdict and scores mean
section: Listening and quality
order: 502
---
Each take gets a sentence first, then the measured scores behind it: voice match, naturalness, prosody and the seam.

The line under a proposal says whose voice it is and whether it sounds like the speaker and sits in the room. Edit it shows the measured tiles: Voice match (SIM-o, wants 0.75), Natural (UTMOS, wants 3.0), Prosody (wants 0.8), Room (wants 0.8). A take below a bar is flagged but can still be added on your word.
