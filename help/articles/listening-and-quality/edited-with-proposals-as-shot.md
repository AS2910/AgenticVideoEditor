Edited plays the video with the lines you added, As shot plays the clip exactly as it was filmed, and With proposals adds what is still waiting for you.

The switch is at the top of the editor; press O to flip between Edited and As shot. The flip keeps your place and keeps playing, so you hear the same moment both ways. Today every change is to the sound, so the picture looks the same in both: the badge on the player says which you are hearing ("As shot · 3 changes hidden", "Edited · 1 proposal not included"). In As shot the transcript follows: added lines dim and say "not in this version", and removed or replaced lines read as they were said. With nothing changed yet there is only one version, and the switch reads "As shot".
