Give feedback on any take or on Voltage's reply with the thumbs beside it, and say why if you like.

**Where.** Each proposal's facts line has a thumb up and a thumb down, and so does Voltage's reply at the end of each turn in the pane.

**A reason.** After a thumb, a small box asks what was good or what was off. It is optional: Skip closes it, Send keeps it. Pressing the same thumb again takes your vote back.

**What it does.** Nothing changes in your video. Votes and reasons help the people who build Voltage see which takes and answers land and which do not.
