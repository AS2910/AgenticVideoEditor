Press ? in the editor to see every shortcut.

↑ ↓ move between lines · Space hears the line or proposal in place · Enter opens the editor · Esc closes it · K adds the proposal · U takes an added line out · A adds a line after · Delete discards or proposes removing · S shifts a line · / puts the cursor in Voltage's box · O flips Edited and As shot · ? opens the sheet.
