---
title: The voice doesn't sound right
section: Troubleshooting
order: 1002
---
Ask for another take, change the delivery, or tell Voltage which voice to use.

Voice it again, a delivery pill, or a sentence like "warmer and slower" or "use Riya Rao" each gets a new take. If the speaker's own voice is off, the clone may be short of clean speech: ten seconds without music is what it wants.
