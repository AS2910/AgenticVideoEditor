Every step Voltage takes is listed with its time and cost, so you can see exactly where the money went.

The trace card under your message shows one line per step: the time since the turn started, the sentence, the tool behind it, and its cost (free when nothing was charged). The Thinking line at the end carries the turn's own cost. Earlier turns fold to a chip; Show all opens a long trace.
