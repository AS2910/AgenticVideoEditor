Each message you send starts a turn: Voltage reads, looks, listens, voices and checks, then tells you what it did and what it cost.

A turn is one message from you and everything Voltage does with it. It can read the transcript, look at frames, listen to the room, search voices, voice a take, measure it and ask you something. The trace lists each step with its tool and cost as it happens. A turn stops at the turn cap (Settings), at a question, or when it has done what you asked.

You can say a goal for the whole video, a change to one line, or an answer to a question. To talk about one line, click it and use Edit it: what you type there goes to Voltage as a sentence.
