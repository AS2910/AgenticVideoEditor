---
title: When Voltage asks you
section: Directing Voltage
order: 202
---
Voltage asks only when it cannot decide, with its own guess as the first option. Saying "go" accepts the guess.

A question appears as a card in the pane with its options. The first option is Voltage's guess; pick one, answer in your own words, or say go. A gate card (Your go is needed) is different: it shows a paid or committing step waiting for your yes, with what that step costs and what the turn has left.
