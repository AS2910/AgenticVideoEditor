Check with me goes ahead on small paid steps and waits for your go on the rest: Voltage voices without asking while a turn spends no more than your limit (Settings › Ask me before spending more than, $0.25 a turn to start), and asks once, on one card, when a step would go past it or would change the video. Questions about what you mean (which line, which voice) are still asked; when a pick leads to a paid step, its price is on the button and picking it is your go. Just do it lets Voltage voice and fix on its own, inside your turn cap.

Open the autonomy word in the editor's header (Check with me ▾) to switch, and to set the turn cap and the effort. Under Just do it, Voltage still asks before the steps you keep in Settings › What still asks: shipping a change, cloning a speaker, designing a voice. Every project can have its own setting; your defaults for new projects live in Settings.
