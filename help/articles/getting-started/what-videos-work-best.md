---
title: What videos work best
section: Getting started
order: 104
---
Clips up to 3 minutes, with clear speech or none at all. One or two speakers work best today.

Voltage works on MP4, MOV and similar files with one video track. Clear speech with little music behind it gives the best transcript and the best clone. A clip with no speech works too: ask for a voice-over and Voltage looks at the picture first. Phone recordings are fine; lip movement is not changed in this build, so a changed line reads best over a cutaway or a wide shot.
