Upload a clip, confirm who is speaking, then tell Voltage the goal in your own words. It proposes changes; you hear them and add the ones you want.

1. Drop a video on Home (up to 10 minutes and 2 GB).
2. Voltage reads it and shows who's speaking with its guess at each role. Press Looks right, or rename a speaker.
3. Say the goal in the box on the right, like "make the offer 30% off".
4. Watch the trace: every step and its cost. Under Check with me, Voltage voices small changes (up to your limit, $0.25 a turn to start) without asking, and asks once before anything bigger; its button says what it will do and the price.
5. The proposal appears on its row in the transcript. Hear it, then Add it. Edit it opens the depth: other deliveries, the facts, the measured scores.
6. Export when it sounds right.
