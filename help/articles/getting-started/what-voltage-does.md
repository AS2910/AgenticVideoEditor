---
title: What Voltage does
section: Getting started
order: 101
---
Voltage changes what a video says without a reshoot: it rewrites lines, voices them in the speaker's own voice, adds voice-overs, and keeps the picture moving.

Upload a clip and Voltage reads it: who speaks, what they say, and when. Tell it what the video should say and it proposes changes line by line. Every proposal is a take you can hear in place before anything goes into the video; nothing is voiced or spent until you say go, and nothing is in the video until you add it.

What it can do today: change the words of a spoken line and voice them in the speaker's own voice or a chosen one; add a line over the picture or where the picture makes room; remove a line and fill it with the room's own sound; move a line to another time; mix an added line under the words; design a voice from a description; export an MP4.
