The three-minute guided run on the sample ad is free and can be replayed any time from this page.

The guided run makes one real edit on our sample ad, in seven clicks. It replays a recorded turn and take, so no vendor is called and nothing is spent. Press Replay the tutorial at the top of Help; Skip tour is always there, and leaving keeps the sample video so you can carry on alone.
