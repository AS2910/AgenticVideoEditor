Your Home starts with a sample project, and a video can come from a share link as well as from a file.

**The sample project.** The first time you open Voltage, Home has a project called **Sample: bhaji-cam ad**. It holds our sample ad with a brief, and its turns replay one recorded run: ask for "Make the offer 30% off" and Voltage reads the line, asks for your go and voices the take, without calling a vendor or spending anything. Delete it whenever you like from its page; it does not come back. If you already had videos or projects, no sample is added.

**Import from a link.** Under the drop zone on Home, paste a share link in **Or paste a link** and choose **Import**. It lands in the project chosen in **Add to**, or as a standalone video. It works with:

- **Google Drive**: open the file, choose Share, set it to anyone with the link, then copy the link.
- **Dropbox**: choose Share, then Copy link.

The file can be up to 2 GB and, like any upload, up to 10 minutes long. While it comes down you see how much has arrived. When it is in, the video opens.

**When an import fails** it says why: the link opens a page instead of a file (usually the file is not shared with anyone who has the link), the file is too big, it is not a video, or the service could not be reached. Fix the sharing and import again.

**YouTube.** Importing a video you own from YouTube needs your Google account connected so Voltage can check it is yours. That is not set up yet; download the video from YouTube Studio and upload the file instead.
