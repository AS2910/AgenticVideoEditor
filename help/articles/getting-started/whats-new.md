What is new in Voltage this week, in short.

**Keep a campaign consistent.** Each brief save is a version, with a history and a diff; a video made with an older brief says "Brief changed" and can be brought up to date. Project words keep how terms are said, banned words and required lines. Check project finds facts that disagree across videos, mixed voices for one role, broken word rules and loudness gaps.

**Work across many videos.** Ask Voltage about all videos in a project at once: one plan, one go, then each video on the batch board. Language versions (Hindi, Hinglish, Tamil, Marathi) stay linked to their master and sync when it changes. Export many videos with presets for YouTube, Reels and Feed in one go, as a zip or a link.

**Review and hand-off.** Videos move through Draft, In review, Changes asked, Approved and Exported on the project board. Send a client a review link: they watch, comment at a time and approve, with no account.

**Reuse and housekeeping.** Deleted things wait in the Bin for 30 days. Pin, archive, search and sort on Home. Save a project as a template. Voltage learns your style from what you add and discard, and suggests one line for the brief at a time.

**Go-live basics.** A sample project to try first, and import from a Google Drive or Dropbox link. Invite people as Editors or Reviewers with their own spend limit. The bell tells you when long work finishes or fails, about review news and spend alerts. Thumbs up or down on any take or reply, with a reason if you like, so we can make Voltage better.

**Billing in test mode.** Plans with monthly credits, top-ups and 5 free credits to start, shown as about how many minutes they buy once that is known. Invoices carry GST for India. No card is asked for and no money moves yet.

**Trust and safety.** A speaker reads a sentence aloud before their voice is cloned, and Withdraw consent now deletes the clone at once. Public figures' voices are refused. Every export carries an invisible mark and a provenance record. Report misuse from any shared link or Help. Download all your data from Settings. Terms, Privacy and Acceptable use are drafts until legal review.
