Voltage runs on credits: one credit pays for one dollar of work, and a plan includes some every month.

**Credits.** Every paid step (transcribing, voicing a line, Voltage's thinking) uses credits at what it costs. New accounts start with 5 free credits. The Billing page shows what is left and, once a minute of finished video has a known cost, about how many minutes that buys.

**Plans and top-ups.** Starter, Studio and Agency each include credits every month; a month's unused plan credits lapse when the month ends. Top-ups add 10, 25 or 50 credits that last 12 months. Spending uses the credits that lapse soonest first. Reviewers on a review link never need a plan.

**When credits run out.** Paid steps pause with a sentence saying so, and nothing already made is lost. You get a note in the bell at 50%, 80% and 100%. Top up or pick a plan to carry on.

**Where you are billed.** India is billed in rupees through Razorpay, with GST on the invoice (CGST and SGST in the same state, IGST across states; add your GSTIN for input credit). Everywhere else is billed in dollars through Stripe. Each payment makes an invoice you can open and print from Billing.

**Test mode.** In this build no card is asked for and no money moves: payments are marked Test mode on the page and on every invoice.
