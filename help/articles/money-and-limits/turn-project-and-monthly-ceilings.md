title: Turn, project and monthly ceilings
section: Money and limits
order: 802
---
Every paid step is checked against your turn cap, the video's ceiling, the project's and your monthly ceiling before it runs.

The turn cap is what one turn may spend. A video has its own ceiling; a project can set one across every video in it; your account has one across everything. The lowest wins, and a step that would pass it waits or stops with a sentence saying so.
