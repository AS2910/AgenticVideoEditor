# Run 5, item 7: Voice tone and feel (2026-10-27)

- Delivery reference: ElevenLabs has no audio style reference for text to speech (multilingual_v2, v3 and v4 take text, settings and text context only), and speech to speech keeps the input's words. So the original line's audio is used two ways: (1) when a line's words do not change (a new voice only), the take IS the original line through the Voice Changer into the new voice (`ElevenLabsVoiceAdapter.speak_like`, billed by the second like `revoice`; a vendor failure falls back to text to speech and says so); (2) when the words change, the original line and the speaker's neighbouring lines are measured; a take outside them is refused and retaken steered back, and the distance ranks the takes. A first take asked in settings that follow the speaker (`prosody.reference_settings`: pitch span 3 st or less gives stability 0.70, 9 st or more gives 0.35 and style 0.20, near-neutral 5 to 7 st leaves the vendor's defaults) is built but OFF by default (`AVE_VOICE_TONE_REFERENCE=1` turns it on): in the eval it gave no delivery gain beyond run-to-run noise and cost about 0.1 to 0.15 UTMOS (see below). Context text (previous/next) is still sent as before.
- What "delivery" is, measured (`app/continuity/prosody.py`, numpy only): pitch register (median, semitones above 100 Hz), pitch span (p90 minus p10 of voiced frames), speaking rate (syllable nuclei a second of speaking time from the loudness envelope; word counts were rejected in Phase 6a as too coarse) and the pitch contour (20 points about the line's own median). Neighbours are the same speaker's lines within 12 s either side, split at 0.3 s pauses, 0.5 s or longer, up to 3 a side; fewer than 2 means no range and no refusal.
- The range a take must sit in: the neighbours' min to max, widened (register plus or minus 2.5 st, span x0.55 to x1.6, rate x0.72 to x1.35). Checked on the eval clip: all 17 of the owl story's own lines sit inside the range of their neighbours, so a take like the speaker passes. Loudness is not a refusal (it is corrected, as before).
- Refusal: a take outside the range fails its check with a plain reason ("Said faster than the lines around it (6.0 syllables a second; they run 4.0 to 5.0).") and is retaken within the existing regeneration loop (`AVE_MAX_REGENERATIONS`, default 2), steered back: speed by at most one 15% step, stability down 0.15 and style up 0.10 when flat, stability up 0.15 when sing-song. If every take is refused, the best is still shown with its reason (as every failed check is today); nothing is hidden. A directed line (a delivery asked for, or Voltage's own voice settings) and an added line are measured but never refused or steered: differing was the point.
- Steered settings go only to the vendor call. The plan kept on the candidate, and the one assessed, is the plan as asked, so a steered take can still be refused and a later edit does not inherit settings nobody chose. The card says "matched to the speaker" under Dials (a fit note carries the numbers).
- Prosody distance = mean of (contour RMS difference to the original line / 3 st) and (|log rate ratio| / log 1.25); 0 is the same delivery, 1 just noticeable. It is a score in the pick (`pipeline._score`, as 1 - 0.2 x distance, so 0.8 at one point, the bar the other scores pass at), and a take refused for delivery ranks below every take that was not. It is stored on the report (`prosody_distance`), shown on the card as "Delivery", and told to Voltage by `measure_take`. Not a pass/fail on its own: two different sentences have different melodies.
- The room on the take: when the speech around the line stands 25 dB or more above the quietest part of the 8 s either side, the take is EQ'd toward the speaker's long-term spectrum (`room.spectral_match`, at most 6 dB a band) and given the room's reverb when one can be read (`room.estimate_reverb`), at proposal time, so what the user hears is what ships. The render then does not add them again (`EditPlan.roomed`; the stems path still lays the bed). A louder bed is left to the render's stems path, as before. `media/room.py` and `media/stems.py` are unchanged (item 8 owns stems on upload).
- Room tone under the new line is laid as a loop with 30 ms equal-power crossfades instead of a hard tile (a click at every repeat before).
- The Voice Changer rung is now fed the dry take (before level, room and room tone), which are put on again when the converted take is assessed; before, a take already carrying room tone went through speech to speech with background removal on.

## Run 5, item 6: fast path for small edits (2026-10-27)

Each: what, why, how to undo.

- The router (`backend/app/agent/fastpath.py`, mirrored in `frontend/src/transcript/fastpath.ts` for the hint only): fast when the edit names one existing spoken line (or the proposal on it), changes 1 to 8 words (for each changed run, the longer of what went and what came, case and punctuation ignored), keeps the voice the editor opened on (the take's voice, else the speaker's chosen voice, else their own; a speaker's clone counts as their own), and no Voltage question is open. Everything else is the full turn with the editor's own sentence, exactly as before: an added line, a new voice, more than 8 words, a delivery-only change (0 words), the tutorial. Undo: `MAX_WORDS`.
- The fast path skips planning: no Claude call at all. It runs the agent's own `synthesize_take` (pre-flight, fit ladder, word rules, line job, spend gates, one line one item), with the line's current voice and the editor's delivery, as a job of kind `turn` so the editor follows it like any turn, Stop and the one-turn-at-a-time rule included. It does not render a preview at the end (a full turn does): the take is heard from its own audio, and "With proposals" renders when asked for. That render is most of the full turn's wall time.
- Spending limit (item 2): a fast take whose estimate is at or under the user's "Ask me before spending more than $X" runs with no approval under Check with me; over it (or with the limit at $0.00, or no consent on record) the edit goes to the full turn, which asks with item 2's single card. Under Just do it it runs (the turn cap still holds). If the tool still asks (a cap), the fast path hands over to the full turn.
- Fallback with a note: a take that does not fit its gap (refused by the pre-flight, or still long after the fit ladder) is not shown; Voltage's pane says "The new words run long for the gap (3.4 s for 2.3 s), so Voltage is taking it from here." and the editor sends its sentence plus that note as a full turn, which rewrites or asks.
